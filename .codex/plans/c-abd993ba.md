Job: `c-abd993ba` · Revision: 1

As a result, users must manually approve decision-complete plans or answer planning questions even when a recommended option is already supplied. ## Risks

- Automatic feedback must be persisted before scheduling the revision; otherwise a restart can leave the job waiting or revise without the selected answers. Use one transactional state transition and recover queued work from the database.
- The planning model may omit a recommendation or return one that is not among the listed options. Do not guess or silently choose another option; preserve the existing clarification workflow.
- A revised plan may repeat questions despite receiving the recommended answers. The persisted one-batch guard prevents unbounded plan revisions and exposes the remaining questions for manual resolution.
- Manual feedback, discard, approval, restart recovery, and the automatic continuation can race. All transitions must use allowed-status predicates and deterministic feedback source IDs rather than unconditional updates.
- Adding a longer callback can exceed Telegram's callback-data limit for long repository names. Preserve the existing short-reference fallback and cover byte length explicitly in tests.
